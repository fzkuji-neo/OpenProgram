App Icon Specification

One mark serves the macOS App, the Windows installer, the WebUI tab, the PWA and the docs site: an indigo tile holding a lavender cell with three flat discs inside. The macOS App ships a flat icon.icns; AppIcon.icon keeps the same artwork as Icon Composer layers for the Liquid Glass toolchain.

The mark

1 · Tile

A diagonal indigo gradient fills the whole tile. In AppIcon.icon it is the linear-gradient fill; flat renders draw it inside the squircle.

+
2 · Cell and discs

The cell is a lavender window with a smooth, radiating wave outline. Three flat discs overlap inside it, back to front: indigo, violet, sky.

→
3 · Flat App icon

The front two discs cast a soft shadow on the disc behind them, which gives the stack depth without sphere lighting.

Geometry and colour

All sizes are fractions of the tile side, measured from the tile centre. The cell outline is the closed curve r(θ) = R · (1 + Σ aₖ cos(kθ + φₖ)) rotated by −15°, with R = 0.364 and four harmonics: k = 9 (a = 0.12, φ = 1.3), k = 7 (0.05, 0.2), k = 12 (0.035, 2.4), k = 2 (0.02, 1.8). The dominant 9th harmonic gives about nine rounded rays of uneven size; the others keep the rays irregular. Every bump and every dip is a smooth arc, so the outline has no cusps. The outline reaches at most 0.446, leaving a clear margin inside the tile.

Asset boundaries

KeepFlat discs in their three colours; the cell's smooth wave outline; the indigo tile gradient; the shadow that separates the front discs from the ones behind.
AvoidSphere lighting or radial highlights on discs, glass translucency, an outline ring around the cell, sharp points in the cell outline, and text or rasters inside the mark.
Icon Composer layersTransparent 1024 × 1024 canvases, one element per layer, no squircle, frame, filter or shadow drawn in the SVG. Shadows come from the group settings.
Flat rendersThe ICNS, favicon and PWA icons are rendered from the flat SVG sources below, never from screenshots of a packaged App.

Checks

apps/desktop/scripts/check-icon.shRun by npm run icon:check. Requires the four layers, exactly three flat discs in the approved colours, the cell fill, the linear-gradient tile fill, and the front-to-back group order with Specular, translucency and glass disabled. Rejects squircles, frames, filters, shadows, text and rasters in the layer SVGs.
openprogram/self_update/control/supervisor.pyThe trusted icon probe renders each of the four layer SVGs with sips and requires a 1024 × 1024 transparent canvas.

The design follows Apple’s App icons HIG and Icon Composer. Packaging follows the Electron Builder icon documentation.
